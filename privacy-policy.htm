Privacy Policy

In the current version of the Apps, we do not collect personal data. If we introduce advertising or analytics SDKs in the future, they may automatically collect the following non-identifying information:

  • device type, model, operating system version;
  • interface language, screen resolution;
  • general usage statistics (e.g., session duration, screens viewed);

2. How we use information

If we begin collecting data, it will be used solely to:

We do not sell or share your personal data with third parties, except where necessary for advertising or analytics services (e.g., Google AdMob, Google Analytics), or where required by law.

Our Apps are not intended for children under 13 (or such other age as may be established by applicable law). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with data, please contact us to have it deleted.
